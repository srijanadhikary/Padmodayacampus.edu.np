The campus offers a supportive learning environment where students can develop their knowledge, skills, and overall personality. 📝 Admissions

Admission Process

Everything you need to know about applying to BBS or BSc at Padmodaya Campus.

👥 BBS Eligibility

Students who have passed +2 or equivalent with a minimum aggregate of D+ and above are eligible to apply for the Management faculty.

🧪 BSc Eligibility

Aggregate score of C and above is required. BSc admission also requires passing the entrance examination conducted by Tribhuvan University.

📄 Required Documents

  • ✓ SEE Marksheet
  • ✓ Plus Two Transcript
  • ✓ Character Certificate
  • ✓ Transfer Certificate
  • ✓ Migration Certificate (Original)
